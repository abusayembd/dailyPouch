Privacy Policy for dailyPouch

Effective Date: September 11, 2026

The Short Version: dailyPouch is a 100% offline and private application. We do not collect, store, or share any of your personal data. Everything stays exactly where it belongs—on your device.

1. Data Collection and Usage

dailyPouch operates entirely offline. The app does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information, financial data, or usage metrics on any external servers. All data you enter into the app (such as budgets, expenses, and pantry items) is saved securely and locally on your device.

2. Third-Party Services

Because dailyPouch does not connect to the internet to function, we do not integrate with third-party analytics trackers, advertising networks, or data collection services. We have no backend servers, meaning a data breach of your information from our end is impossible.

3. Permissions

dailyPouch respects your privacy and only requests the absolute minimum permissions required for the app to function properly on your device. The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, or microphone.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data belongs to you. Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control over it. You can delete all your data at any time simply by clearing the app's data in your device settings or uninstalling dailyPouch.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If any changes are made, we will update the "Effective Date" at the top of this page. However, our core commitment to keeping dailyPouch 100% offline and private will never change.
