1. Overview & Philosophy

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application DailyPouch: Offline Life Tools (hereinafter referred to as the "App"), developed by Abu Sayem Sarkar.

The App was engineered on an offline-first, privacy-by-default architecture. We believe your daily budget, personal expenses, utility subscriptions, pantry goods, and workouts are strictly personal. Consequently, the App functions autonomously without requiring network connectivity, user registration, account creation, or external server communication.

2. Data Collection and Processing

We collect zero personal data. Specifically:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, or biometric records.
  • No Financial Records: Any budgets, subscription fees, pantry purchases, price calculations, and cash flow records you input remain strictly local to your device.
  • No Tracking or Diagnostics: We do not use behavioral tracking SDKs, ad networks, device fingerprinting, or automated telemetry.
  • No Location Data: The App does not access, collect, or monitor GPS or network-based location data.

3. Local Storage and Data Security

All user data created across the App's modules (Subscription Tracker, Smart Unit Price Calculator, Pantry & Medicine Expiry Tracker, Expense Tracker, Envelope Budgeter, and Interval Workout Timer) is written directly to the operating system's private sandboxed internal storage (via standard local device files).

Because no data ever traverses an external network or resides on third-party cloud infrastructure, remote server-side breaches or unauthorized third-party access to your data through our services is architecturally impossible. Your data is protected by your device's physical security, PIN, and operating system encryption.

4. Device Permissions & Hardware Capabilities

DailyPouch adheres to the principle of least privilege. We only request the minimum device permissions necessary to perform offline functions:

🔔 Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)
Used solely to deliver scheduled reminders directly from your operating system (such as impending pantry expiration dates or upcoming subscription bill renewals). These notifications are generated locally on-device without any internet requests.
⏱️ Keep Screen Awake (WAKE_LOCK)
Used strictly within the Interval & Workout Timer module to prevent your screen from turning off while actively running a workout routine.
📤 User-Initiated Export & Sharing
If you choose to export summaries or receipts (such as generating a PDF report or sharing a calculation), the App creates the document locally in temporary memory. Sharing occurs solely via the native operating system share sheet triggered explicitly by you.

5. Third-Party Services and Advertising

DailyPouch contains no advertisements and integrates with no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs (such as Google AdMob, Meta Audience Network, Firebase Analytics, Flurry, or AppsFlyer). The App operates independently of any commercial data exchange or ad-serving network.

6. Data Retention, Control, and Deletion

You retain 100% control over all data stored within DailyPouch:

  • Modifying & Deleting Entries: You can edit or permanently delete individual expenses, subscriptions, or pantry items at any time within each respective tool.
  • Purging All App Data: You can erase all stored information instantly at the OS level by navigating to your device's Settings > Apps > DailyPouch > Storage > Clear Data / Clear Storage.
  • App Uninstallation: Uninstalling DailyPouch automatically and permanently deletes all locally stored data, databases, and preferences associated with the App from your device.

7. Children's Online Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

DailyPouch is safe for general audiences and complies fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Article 8 of the GDPR. Because DailyPouch does not collect, store, or share any personal data from any user, it does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union).

8. Global Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Under legal frameworks such as the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA):

  • Right to Access & Portability: All your data is accessible to you at all times directly on your device.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): You can delete all your records at any time without needing to request permission or submit a ticket.
  • "Do Not Sell My Personal Information": We do not sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your personal data to any third party for monetary or commercial consideration.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app enhancements or legal adjustments. Any modifications will be posted directly to this public web page with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp. We recommend reviewing this page periodically for any changes.
